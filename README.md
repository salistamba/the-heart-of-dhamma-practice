# The Heart of Dhamma Practice

**A Restoration of the Buddha’s Original Teaching Based on the Suttas of the Pāli Canon.**

This book is devoted to the study of the Dhamma and the practice of liberation. It draws on the suttas of the Pāli Canon and direct investigation of experience. The central task of practice is to see conditioned processes as they are: their impermanence, unsatisfactoriness, and not-self nature, as well as the causes and conditions of their arising and cessation. Particular attention is given to ignorance, craving, clinging, and becoming, and to recognizing them in everyday life.

The book covers the four `ariyasacca`, the Noble Eightfold Path, ethical conduct (`sīla`), mindfulness (`sati`), `satipaṭṭhāna`, collectedness (`samādhi`), the jhānas and `arūpa-āyatana`, dependent arising (`paṭiccasamuppāda`), the three characteristics (`anicca`, `dukkha`, `anattā`), the five `khandhā`, the six sense spheres, `taṇhā`, `upādāna`, `bhava`, `saṅkhārā`, `papañca`, `nirodha`, and `Nibbāna`.

**Status:** the book is a work in progress and continues to be expanded and refined. The text may contain inaccuracies and errors of formulation. A glossary of Pāli terms and links to the source texts are provided at the end of the PDF.

This text is not intended as medical, psychological, or psychotherapeutic advice.
