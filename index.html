The Heart of Dhamma Practice

A restoration of the Buddha's Original Teaching based on the suttas of the Pāli Canon.

This book is devoted to the Dhamma and the practice of liberation. It is based on the suttas of the Pāli Canon and direct investigation of experience.

The revision history is available on GitHub.

About the book

The aim of practice is to see conditioned processes as they are: their impermanence (anicca), unsatisfactoriness (dukkha), not-self nature (anattā), and the causes and conditions of their arising and cessation. Particular attention is given to ignorance (avijjā), craving (taṇhā), clinging (upādāna), and becoming (bhava), including in everyday life.

The book covers the Four Noble Truths (ariyasacca), the Noble Eightfold Path, ethical conduct (sīla), mindfulness (sati), satipaṭṭhāna, collectedness (samādhi), the jhānas and arūpa-āyatana, dependent arising (paṭiccasamuppāda), the five khandhā, the six sense spheres, saṅkhārā, papañca, nirodha, and Nibbāna.

Status of the book

The text is a work in progress and continues to be expanded and refined. It may contain inaccuracies and errors of formulation.

This book is not intended as medical, psychological, or psychotherapeutic advice.
